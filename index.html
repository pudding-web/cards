Math
What is carrying capacity in a logistic model?
Answer

The maximum population size an environment can support (the model’s “cap”).

Math
What makes a growth function “logistic” instead of exponential?
Answer

Growth slows as it nears a carrying capacity, often written as c / (1 + ae^{-kt}).

Progressive Era
Why did Progressives oppose political machines?
Answer

They promoted corruption and patronage, making officials less accountable to voters.

Constitution
Which amendment established direct election of U.S. senators?
Answer

The 17th Amendment.

Reform
Which president used the Sherman Antitrust Act as a “trustbuster”?
Answer

Theodore Roosevelt.

Prohibition
Which amendment banned the sale of alcohol (Prohibition)?

The 18th Amendment.

Which amendment gave women the right to vote nationwide?

Answer

The growth rate (how fast the population increases when it’s far below K).
